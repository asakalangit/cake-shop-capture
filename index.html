How to Add This Tool to Your Canva Website

This page adjusts cleanly to fit inside a Canva Website frame on phones, tablets, and laptops. Follow these simple steps to put it on your shop’s Canva site:

1

Put This Page Online

Upload this single web page file to any free host (such as Netlify Drop, GitHub Pages, or Vercel) so it has a live web link.

2

Open Your Canva Site

In Canva, open your website design and click Apps on the left toolbar. Type Embed in the search box and open it.

3

Paste Your Web Link

Paste your live page link into Canva’s Embed box and tap Add to design. Stretch the frame wide so staff can see the whole table.

4

Publish & Bookmark

Publish your Canva Website and bookmark the link on your counter tablet or phone. Your list saves automatically between visits.
